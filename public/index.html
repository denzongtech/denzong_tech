Privacy, AI and service limitations

Contact and stay enquiry forms are intended to be processed by Netlify Forms when the site is deployed and form detection is active. The site owner should review submissions, limit access and configure deletion/retention practices. Avoid collecting unnecessary information.

AI requests are sent to the configured Google Gemini API through the Netlify Function. The API key stays on the server when configured correctly; never put it in this HTML. Provider usage, retention, free-tier and paid-tier terms may differ and may change. Check current terms before using real customer or confidential business data.

Denzong Stay is an enquiry flow only. No live rates, verified accommodation inventory, automated reservation, customer account, payment gateway, tax calculation, refund system or partner dashboard is included in this starter project. Do not describe an enquiry as confirmed. Property owners must verify listings and confirm availability and terms.

This page is a starter privacy notice, not a substitute for a complete privacy policy or professional legal review. Before commercial launch, prepare appropriate terms, privacy practices, customer support and service-provider agreements.
